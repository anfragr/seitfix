FAQ

Questions & answers

What's not included in the € 599 price?+
The price covers a simple one-pager with a clear scope. Not included: multi-page sites, online shops, special features (booking, calendars, member areas), logo/graphic design and extensive SEO. Those are available separately on request.
What is a one-pager?+
A website on a single page. All key info – services, benefits, contact – built compactly. Visitors scroll instead of clicking. Ideal to convince quickly and clearly.
Who is Seitfix for?+
For tradespeople, service providers and small businesses that want a simple, professional website without a big agency process and want to win new customers.
Is hosting mandatory?+
No. The hosting plan (€ 30/month) is optional. With hosting we handle domain, maintenance and small changes. Without it, you get all files to publish yourself.
Who owns the finished website?+
The finished website is yours. You get all files. Cancel hosting and you can host anywhere, anytime.
Is VAT charged?+
No. Under § 6 (1) 27 Austrian VAT Act (small-business rule) no VAT is charged. All prices are final.
