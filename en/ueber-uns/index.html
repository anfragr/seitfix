About us

Digital products that are genuinely needed

Seitfix is a small company from Graz that builds digital products that are genuinely needed – rather than as many as possible.

What interests us is what makes life a little better or easier for the people we build for.

Our current passion project is Retteria, an app that rescues leftover food from restaurants before it ends up in the bin – fair for the hospitality trade and good for the city.
